Auto Process Notes

Prototype · not connected to Pega

Today, getting a case's notes means typing something like “What are the Pre Assessment notes for TSLA?” into the assistant every time — so more often than not, nobody does. Here, opening the case and choosing Pre Assessment or Process asks that question for you and shows the answer right away.

1

Case is opened. Its client ID (ticker) comes from case metadata — already on every case today.

2

Pick Pre Assessment or Process, and the assistant matches that choice to the correct notes source, instead of an analyst searching for it by hand.

3

Notes render right in the case view, with the source cited — the same link an analyst would have opened themselves.
